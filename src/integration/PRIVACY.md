# The 12th Interface – Privacy Policy

The 12th Interface is a student class project (CSCE 634, Texas A&M University).

- **What we collect:** if you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address. We also save the sleep, energy and study-preference answers you enter so the planner can use them.
- **How we use it:** only to run the study planner for you. We do not sell, share or use it for ads.
- **Where it is stored:** on the app's hosting (Streamlit Community Cloud) and its database.
- **Deleting your data:** email harshinikondepudi@tamu.edu and we will delete your account and data.

Contact: harshinikondepudi@tamu.edu
